Implementation
NYC Reads phases
Districts adopted the NYC Reads curricula in successive waves. Because the waves began in different school years, districts had completed different numbers of implementation years by the 2026 test. This page groups districts by wave and reports results for each group.
Read this before using this page. Districts were not assigned to phases at random, and the groups did not start from the same place. Differences below are descriptive contrasts between groups of districts, not estimates of the effect of NYC Reads. Nothing here isolates the program from everything else that changed in those districts over the same years.
NYC Reads launch timeline, by district
Launch timeline as published by NYCPS. District 75 appears in several phases but is not reported in the district file, so it cannot be shown here.

Implementation partners
Professional learning providers and curriculum
Districts grouped by the NYC Reads professional learning provider (JESP) they worked with and by the curriculum they adopted in school year 2025–26, the year the spring 2026 test measures. K–5 and grades 6–8 assignments are held separately because they differ.
These groups were not formed for comparison. Districts were not assigned to providers or curricula at random and did not start from the same place, group sizes are very uneven, and several districts work with more than one provider. Differences between groups describe the districts in them; they do not measure provider or curriculum effectiveness.

Comparability
Paper and computer administration
NYSED moved the ELA test from paper to computer one set of grades at a time. Grades are grouped here by the year in which they transitioned, and results are reported for each group.
What this can and cannot tell you. Grades differ from one another for many reasons that have nothing to do with how the test was delivered, and each group here is only two grades. This page shows that the timing of the switch lines up with a movement in the results; it does not establish that the format caused it.
